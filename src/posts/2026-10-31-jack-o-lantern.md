> The title is just because today is Halloween. There’s no other meaning behind it.

It’s been a long time since I last sat down with a clear mind to work on something. AI has taken over both life and work. I want to write something, but I don’t know what to write. Over the past two months, I’ve spent some time rebuilding my personal website, and it was only today that I finally finished it. I thought I ought to write something to mark the occasion, and so this post came to be.

### The website?

I can’t say it was 100% built by hand. That wouldn’t be realistic. But most of the site’s design is my own. The process wasn’t exactly smooth. Far too much time went into filing issues and fixing bugs upstream. After all these years, the frontend ecosystem is still as chaotic as ever. I find myself missing the days of Webpack and ESLint a little. They weren’t fast, but at least things didn’t feel quite so restless.

### How have things been?

Not good, not bad. Still working, still employed. I’ve barely written any code by hand these past few months. AI writes it, then AI reviews it. I’m not sure whether this is a good direction, but it probably is the future, and I still feel powerless to do anything about it. Nobody cares about implementation details anymore, or technical debt. They’re all pinning their hopes on the next generation of AI models. Until the bubble bursts.

### The future?

I don’t know. Work is getting less and less interesting, and so is life. I feel less and less like talking to other people. Too many people around me believe AI will eventually replace everything. I don’t want to argue with them. There’s no point. Is this really any different from the people who were pushing the Metaverse? Let time tell.

### An ending?

There isn’t much else to say. I’ll leave it here. Oh, right.

> Trick or treat.
